My Daily Fitness Routine

Morning Cardio

Duration: 30 mins

Includes jogging, jumping jacks, and light stretching to boost energy for the day.

Core Strength

Duration: 20 mins

Includes planks, crunches, and leg raises to strengthen your core muscles.

Upper Body

Duration: 25 mins

Includes push-ups, dumbbell presses, and pull-ups for building strength.

Lower Body

Duration: 25 mins

Includes squats, lunges, and calf raises to tone legs and improve balance.

Yoga & Recovery

Duration: 30 mins

Includes deep breathing and full-body stretches to relax muscles and prevent injury.
