🏃 Fuel Up with Fun Movement!

Active play builds strong bones, boosts your immune system, and sharpens your mind for learning! Staying active each day keeps your brain alert and gives you plenty of natural energy.

Riding a bike, jumping rope, playing tag, or dancing in your room — any activity that gets you moving keeps your body strong and healthy!

🌟 Super Health Habits

  • 🥧 Choose colorful meals filled with fruits & veggies
  • 🩹 Keep a water bottle handy and stay hydrated all day
  • 🌓 Rest well each night to rebuild your daily energy
  • 🤸 Take active play breaks away from electronic screens
