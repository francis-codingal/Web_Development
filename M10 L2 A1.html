🚀 Wonders of Space

Step into the universe! Here are some mind-blowing facts about space and astronomy.

🌌 Fact 1: A Day on Venus

A single day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. It takes Venus 243 Earth days to rotate once on its axis, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

Why does this happen?

Venus has an extremely slow backward rotation, meaning the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east!

🪐 Fact 2: Saturn's Rings

Saturn’s rings are extraordinarily wide, stretching up to 282,000 kilometres, yet they are extremely thin—mostly just 10 metres thick!

What are the rings made of?

The rings consist of billions of tiny chunks of ice, rock, and dust floating together in space.

Could you stand on Saturn?

No! Saturn is a gas giant made almost entirely of hydrogen and helium, so there is no solid surface to land on.

☀️ Fact 3: The Solar Wind

The Sun blows out a continuous stream of charged particles known as solar wind at speeds of around 400 kilometres per second.

What creates the Auroras?

When these charged particles interact with Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere, they create glowing light displays known as Northern and Southern Lights.

Fun comparison

It takes light from the Sun about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth, traveling at 300,000 kilometres per second!

Facts sourced from NASA and ESA Public Archives.
