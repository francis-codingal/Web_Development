Morning Cadet Routine

  1. Complete morning anti-gravity fitness exercises.
  2. Inspect flight gear and space suit seals.
  3. Enjoy a nutrient-dense space breakfast.
  4. Report to the launch bay for morning briefing.
  5. Attend zero-gravity simulation training.

Features of the Space Academy

  • Virtual reality space simulator
  • Hydro-training zero-gravity pool
  • Astronomy observation dome
  • Robotics and rover lab
  • Hydroponic space garden

Academy Specializations

Astrophysics
The study of stars, black holes, nebulae, and the physical laws governing the universe.
Robotics & Engineering
Designing, building, and programming autonomous planetary rovers and spacecraft systems.
Astrobiology
Exploring the possibilities of life on other planets and studying extremophile organisms.
Space Navigation
Learning orbital mechanics, star mapping, and piloting spacecraft through complex routes.
