Exploring Space — Quick Guide

The universe is filled with incredible mysteries, stars, and celestial wonders. Here is a quick guide using lists!

Steps to Stargaze at Night:

  1. Find a dark spot away from city lights
  2. Allow your eyes 15 minutes to adjust to the darkness
  3. Use a star map app to locate constellations
  4. Bring a pair of binoculars or a small telescope
  5. Keep track of the moon phase before planning your night

Incredible Cosmic Wonders:

  • Olympus Mons — the largest volcano in the solar system, located on Mars
  • Milky Way Galaxy — home to over 100 billion stars, including our Sun
  • Jupiter's Great Red Spot — a massive storm that has been raging for centuries
  • Andromeda Galaxy — the closest major spiral galaxy to our own

Key Terms:

Light-Year
The distance light travels in one year, roughly 9.46 trillion kilometers
Exoplanet
A planet that orbits a star outside our solar system
Nebula
A giant cloud of dust and gas in space where new stars are born
